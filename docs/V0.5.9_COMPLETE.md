## Job 92 root cause

yt-dlp **exit 0** did not mean FrameForge found the file. `--print` paths were checked with `Path(item).is_file()` (relative to GUI cwd, not `FrameForge\youtube`); lines containing ` at ` or starting with `[` were dropped; `--download-archive` recorded the id **before** our lookup, so retries skipped with no new file. That `FileNotFoundError` became **`unknown`** and fail-paused with Firefox. Cookies were never the fix.
